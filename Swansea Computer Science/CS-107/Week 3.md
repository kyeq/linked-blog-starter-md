### Data compression
- Reduce the amount of bits a file contains 

(the size of the compressed data divided by the side of the original data)

$$
compression\_ratio = \frac{length (compressed)}{length (original)}
$$
A data compression, can be:
- lossless
	- which means the original data can be retrieved without loss of original information
- lossy
	- which means some information may be lost in the process of compression
