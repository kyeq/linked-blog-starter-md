# Population Growth Examples
> [!info] From the CS-128 notes on Canvas — [open the original page](https://canvas.swansea.ac.uk/courses/68227/pages/population-growth-examples)

These are the two population growth examples - use  **while**  when you don't know how many times you are going to loop (in this case, you want to count the number of years it takes for the population to double). Use  **for**  when you do know (what is the population after 10 years)? The idea here is similar to the interest rate examples.
