# 4.0 Introduction
> [!info] From the CS-128 notes on Canvas — [open the original page](https://canvas.swansea.ac.uk/courses/68227/pages/4-dot-0-introduction)

In this chapter we'll look at  *choice and decisions -* getting programs to do one thing or another (or choose between more than two options) based on tests (or  *conditions* ) where we, say, compare two numbers and do one thing if one is greater and something else if the other is greater. We will look at  *if statements* ; the extended version of them called  *if-else statements* ; and also  *switch statements.*

As will all chapters, in the summary you'll find a self-test quiz: I'm not going to look at the marks you get for these (they definitely do not count towards your score for the module). But I will check to see if you're actually doing them.

Also at the end of this chapter you'll find a few exercises for you to attempt - please do so as you need to practice to become a good programmer.
