# 15.1 Introduction
> [!info] From the CS-128 notes on Canvas — [open the original page](https://canvas.swansea.ac.uk/courses/68227/pages/15-dot-1-introduction)

In this chapter we're going to return to the subject of software engineering - and (mainly) look at  *agile* methodologies and the  *Unified Modelling Language.* It's only going to be an introduction and you will do this in more detail later in the programme.

#### The Tricky Part About Software Engineering

Many things in Computer Science (and other areas of Science and Engineering) can be tricky to learn, but once you have learned them, you can just do them. E.g. at some point you are going to do  *complexity theory* and understand how that relates to how 'hard' problems are to solve, and how that relates to 'big-Oh' notation. Pretty tricky for many people, but once you've got it, you can do it. Software Engineering isn't like that - it's  *really easy* to understand what's going on as it's not conceptually complex. But it's  *really hard* to be able to actually do it to start with until you have  *experience.*
