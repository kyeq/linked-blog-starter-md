##### Who is Richard Feynman?
**Richard Feynman**
[![Feynman, smiling|176](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/4/42/Richard_Feynman_Nobel.jpg/250px-Richard_Feynman_Nobel.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Richard_Feynman_Nobel.jpg)

Richard Feynman was an American theoretical physicist who won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1965. He shared the 1965 Nobel Prize with Julian Schwinger and Shin'ichiro Tomonaga for work on how light and matter interact (Quantum Electrodynamics). He also created simple drawings to show how subatomic particles interact, helping others beter understand hard math problems and make them easier to solve. Finally, he helped explain superfluidity (liquid helium flowing without friction) and proposed the parton model in particle physics.
