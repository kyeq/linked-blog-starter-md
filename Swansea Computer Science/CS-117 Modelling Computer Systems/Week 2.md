### Tautologies and Contradictions
A formula *p* is:
- A **tautology** if, and only if, it is true under all interpretations
	- In this case, we say p is **valid**

- A contradiction if, and only if, it is false under all interpretations
	- In this case, we say p is **unsatisfiable**

- **Satisifiable** if, and only if, it is true under some interpretations
	- i.e., it is *not* a contradiction
